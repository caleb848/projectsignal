About ProjectSignal

ProjectSignal is an experimental project management product exploring how creative and marketing teams can better visualize project health, dependencies, risks, approvals and launch readiness.

Most project management tools are excellent at showing what work exists. This experiment asks a slightly different question: what does a project manager need to know to decide where attention is required?

The answer here is a small set of rules applied consistently. They cover schedule buffer, delays travelling through dependencies, items waiting on other people, budget forecast, team capacity and open risks. Each is combined into a health score whose working is shown.

What I wanted to explore

  • Moving beyond basic task tracking

    Task lists say what exists. The questions that matter are what is late, what it affects, and what has to happen next.

  • Making dependencies visible

    A late approval matters because of what sits downstream of it. The dependency engine turns that chain into dates.

  • Surfacing risks earlier

    Waiting items and buffer consumption are leading indicators. They show up before a milestone goes red.

  • Reducing approval bottlenecks

    The Waiting Room shows who holds each item, how long it has been there, and whether it has been escalated.

  • Improving executive reporting

    The same facts, pitched at three levels of detail for executives, clients and the delivery team.

  • Using AI responsibly in project operations

    Where logic can be explicit, it is. Nothing is presented as a black box, and every statement traces back to data.

How it works

  • • A fixed demo date (Wednesday, October 7, 2026) anchors every calculation, so the scenarios stay consistent.
  • • Milestones form a dependency graph. Delays flow downstream, using contingency first, then compressing QA, then moving the launch.
  • • Health scores, insights and briefs are generated from that data by explicit rules. See how health is scored.

Built as a personal learning experiment with Claude Code.
